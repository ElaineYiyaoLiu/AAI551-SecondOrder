\section{Candidate structural analysis}
Fix a common adoption path, remove dividends, and restrict to an unclipped branch
with employment below 95. Write $E_t=k_tD_t$ and $L_t=l_tE_t$, with positive
coefficients determined by adoption and common scenario inputs. Benefits are
$0.12(95-E_t)$ and $C_t=0.75[L_t+0.12(95-E_t)+u]$, where $u$ is annual UBI in
thousand USD. On this branch, with $D_t=1+\alpha(C_{t-1}/45-1)$,
\[
\frac{\partial C_t}{\partial u}=0.75\left[1+(l_t-0.12)k_t\frac{\alpha}{45}
\frac{\partial C_{t-1}}{\partial u}\right].
\]
Common policy-independent initial consumption implies zero employment response
in year one. Nonnegative feedback and $l_t\geq0.12$ imply nonnegative consumption
response while this branch remains valid. This is a candidate derivation awaiting
independent verification, branch checks, and related-work assessment.
